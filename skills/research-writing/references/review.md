# Review a research revision

Scale the record to the task. A local repair needs a concise note.
A full article needs an argument map, a claim record and a verified change record.

## Distinct verdicts

Record pass, revise, unchecked or not applicable for each relevant dimension:

| Dimension | Question |
|---|---|
| Source fidelity | Did the revision preserve the intended method, evidence and contribution? |
| Scientific validity | Do the claims follow under the actual assumptions? |
| Argument | Does the reader understand why the work and its parts matter? |
| Exposition | Can the intended reader follow the definitions and mechanism? |
| Presentation | Do the rendered pages, figures and references support the account? |
| Author acceptance | Has the author accepted this exact revision? |

Give a locator and reason for each revise or unchecked verdict.
A scientific blocker keeps the affected claim open.

## Critic finding

Use this compact format:

- Passage and locator.
- Problem, evidence and severity.
- Smallest adequate action: keep, edit, move, narrow, defer or ask.

Report missing evidence and missing contributions.
Preserve a useful qualification even when a phrase checker flags it.
Resolve conflicting findings before rewriting.
A count of findings supplies no measure of critic quality.

## Final artifact checks

Compare the final source with the baseline.
Check changed values, assumptions, citations and figure identities.
Check references after moving definitions or proofs.
Build the final source and inspect the output at its intended reading size.
Review figure labels, table width, page gaps and the placement of supporting evidence.
Record the source revision and the inspected output.

## Evaluation of the skill

Use the same task, input, tools and resource limits in both conditions.
Keep expected decisions away from the assistant that performs the task.
A reviewer should compare anonymised outputs where possible.
Save outputs and actual calculations before recording a verdict.
Check claim preservation and scientific errors before assessing prose preference.
Report sample size, disagreements and missing telemetry.
Passing a synthetic case does not establish general improvement.
If a baseline already passes, report that fact.

Use development documents to find defects.
Use fresh documents for claims about transfer to unseen work.
Keep private artifacts outside a public package and its Git history.
Publication requires the author's release decision.
