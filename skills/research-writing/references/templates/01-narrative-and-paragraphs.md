# Templates: narrative brief and paragraph map

Reusable working template. Select the fields required by the task. Keep original source material in the authorised working record.

Use only the fields needed for the task. A short edit may need a few lines.

## Review entry conditions

Link the completed contribution inventory and definition inventory before a manuscript critic review.
Keep any unfilled required field visible as an evidence gap.
Record the current author's permission and the exact scope of permitted edits.

## Section and translation use

For a lay abstract, move from field motivation to the obstacle, contribution and principal result.
Name the framework's contribution at the audience's level of detail.
Preserve its expressivity, training and benchmark scope.
Follow the author's chosen ending; a discussion can hold a concrete future study.

For a translation, compare paragraph jobs, assumptions and result scope with the reviewed source version.
Record acceptance for the translated version separately.
