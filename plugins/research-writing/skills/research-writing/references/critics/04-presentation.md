# Critic: figures, citations and rendered pages

Reusable guide. This guide transfers an earlier writing method. Its review judgements have no independent accuracy benchmark.

## Task

Inspect the built document at the intended reading size.
Use the source revision and the figure manifest to identify exactly what changed.

## Tests

1. Check each figure's scientific purpose and its distance from the relevant explanation.
2. Check label size, panel order, axes, legend and caption scope.
3. Compare result values and figure assets with their approved sources.
4. Check formal environments, proof endings and cross-reference destinations.
5. Inspect changed pages and their neighbours for clipping, blank areas and poor breaks.
6. Check citation identity, rendered links and bibliography completeness.

A source float directive provides no evidence of the final placement.
Report printed and PDF page numbers from the inspected file.
Keep unavoidable chapter-opening blanks separate from layout defects.

## Verdict

Name the page, object and visible defect.
Recommend a bounded layout change that preserves the content and legibility.
Report unavailable source assets or pages as unchecked.

Successful compilation, font checks and link checks each establish a specific technical result.
Keep their verdict separate from scientific validity and author acceptance.

## Caption and asset preservation

State the scientific point before the figure reference.
Keep panel details in the caption unless the argument needs them.
Retain axes, statistics, uncertainty and conditions needed to interpret each plot.
Check a caption again when its model, plot or theorem scope changes.

Split a composite display when its plots support separate claims and placement improves their use.
Preserve the data and readable labels after the split.
Combine related stages into panels when that helps readers follow a mechanism.
Use the approved result assets unless the author authorises a replacement.
A nearby local run needs its own version and purpose record.

Record the rendered file identity and refresh page locators after the final build.
Report editorial residue, placeholders and accidental review markers as located presentation findings.
Keep intended review markers and acceptance status consistent with the author brief.
