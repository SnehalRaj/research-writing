## What the transfer preserves

Each rule retains its action, triggering condition, important exception and check.
The teaching cases retain the failure mechanism and the content that a repair must preserve.
Each case identifies its synthetic status.
The templates retain fields for scientific scope, evidence, exact author responses and accepted scope.
The preparation stage precedes a narrative, rewrite or expert critic assignment.

The private audit records per-source hashes, section locators, destinations and adaptation reasons.
It covers all 46 files in the source retrospective and the additional style sources.
Source feedback and manuscript quotations remain private.
Institutional procedures, old deadlines and personal details do not become universal article-writing instructions.

## How to check the transfer

Check semantic coverage against the source conditions, rather than counting headings alone.
Test whether a fresh assistant can retrieve and apply the detailed guidance.
Verify that both platform packages contain the same references and usable relative links.
Keep source coverage, distribution tests and comparative writing quality as separate results.
The public evaluation record states the checks and their limits.
